Engineered from the ground up.

Our own hardware and our own model. Six core components turn a hand gesture into a spoken Arabic sentence — entirely on the device.

  • 5 flex sensors, one per finger
  • 6-axis motion tracker
  • On-board micro computer
  • Custom ML model, multi-signer
  • Built-in speaker
  • Lithium battery, safety-first

Two-way conversation, one companion app.

The Turjuman app turns the other person's speech into instant text — in Arabic and English, online or offline — completing the conversation loop while keeping every word on the device.

  • Arabic & English speech-to-text
  • Works online and offline
  • Locally stored conversations
